# 🎬 CinePOS Feature Walkthrough - Video Script

**Duration:** 3 - 5 Minutes
**Target Audience:** Theater Owners, Managers, and POS Operators

---

## Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:30)
**Visual:** Cinematic zoom-in on the CinePOS logo followed by the Dashboard.
**Narration:**
"Welcome to CinePOS — the modern, production-grade POS and theater management system designed to streamline your cinema operations. Whether you're running a single screen or a multi-theater chain, CinePOS brings power and simplicity to your box office."

---

## Scene 2: Authentication & RBAC (0:30 - 1:00)
**Visual:** Show the login screen. Demonstrate logging in as 'Super Admin' using the quick-login buttons. Show the different navigation items available to an Admin versus a Clerk.
**Narration:**
"Security is at our core. CinePOS features a robust JWT-based authentication system with four distinct roles: Super Admin, Theater Owner, Manager, and Clerk. Role-Based Access Control ensures that your staff only sees what they need to. Clerks focus on sales, while Managers and Admins handle the heavy lifting of scheduling and analytics."

---

## Scene 3: Movie & Show Management (1:00 - 1:45)
**Visual:** Navigate to 'Movies'. Show adding a new movie. Then navigate to 'Shows' and demonstrate scheduling a show. Point out the conflict detection and dynamic pricing (VIP, Premium, Regular).
**Narration:**
"Managing your content is effortless. Add movies with full metadata, from ratings to duration. Scheduling shows is just as simple. Our intelligent scheduler detects conflicts and allows for dynamic pricing per show. Set your VIP, Premium, and Regular rates in seconds, and watch your schedule come to life."

---

## Scene 4: The POS Terminal - The Heart of CinePOS (1:45 - 3:00)
**Visual:** Open the 'POS' page. Select a show. Show the interactive seat map. Click on seats to lock them. Show the 5-minute timer. Apply a coupon (e.g., FIRST50). Enter customer details and complete a booking. Show the generated ticket.
**Narration:**
"The heart of CinePOS is our high-performance POS terminal. The interactive seat map provides real-time availability with 10-second polling. When a customer selects a seat, it's locked instantly across all terminals for five minutes, preventing double bookings. Applying coupons is a breeze — just enter the code and watch the discount apply. Complete the booking with multiple payment methods and generate a professional, QR-coded ticket instantly."

---

## Scene 5: Analytics & Reports (3:00 - 4:00)
**Visual:** Navigate to the 'Analytics' dashboard. Hover over the Recharts graphs (Revenue, Bookings, Occupancy). Then show the 'Reports' page and the different filters.
**Narration:**
"Data-driven decisions are easy with our built-in analytics. Monitor your revenue, bookings, and occupancy in real-time with beautiful, interactive charts. Need a deeper dive? Our reports section provides detailed breakdowns of movie performance, cancellation trends, and daily revenue, all ready for export or printing."

---

## Scene 6: Desktop Experience & Conclusion (4:00 - 5:00)
**Visual:** Show the Tauri desktop app interface (if possible, or describe the system tray and native window controls).
**Narration:**
"CinePOS isn't just a web app. Our native Tauri desktop application provides a faster, more integrated experience with system tray support and native print controls. Built with Next.js, Prisma, and Rust, CinePOS is fast, secure, and ready to scale. Take control of your cinema today with CinePOS."
